# Quickly Understand Market Charts

Understand current market conditions to help make precise investment strategies. Trade data.

Currently, only trading pairs on the Endless chain are supported.
1. **Basic K-line (Candlestick) Information**

    - Logos and names of the two tokens
    - Click to open a dropdown menu to select different trading pairs
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/understanding-charts/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2. **K-line (Candlestick) Data Overview**  
    All data corresponds to the currently selected trading pair:

    - **Price**: Real-time exchange rate of the base token against the quote token
    - **4H Change (%)**: Red indicates a price decrease, green indicates an increase
    - **USD Price**: Real-time USD price of the base token
    - **224H High**: Highest price of the base token in the past 24 hours
    - **24H Low**: Lowest price of the base token in the past 24 hours
    - **24H Trading Volume (Token)**: Amount of base token traded in the past 24 hours
    - **24H Trading Volume (USD)**: USD value of trading volume in the past 24 hours
    - **24H Number of Trades**: Total number of transactions in the past 24 hours
    - **24H Fee**: Total trading fees generated by the liquidity pool in the past 24 hours
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/understanding-charts/media/image2.png" alt="" /></p>

3. **Trade List**

    - Transaction time
    - Type: Based on the base token, including Buy and Sell
    - Value: USD value of the transaction
    - Base token amount: "-" indicates sell, "+" indicates buy
    - Quote token amount: "-" indicates sell, "+" indicates buy
    - Transaction hash
