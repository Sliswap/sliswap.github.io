# Endless Chain Swap

1. **Connect your Web3 wallet.**

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2. **Select the token you want to 'pay' with.**

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image2.png" alt="" /></p>
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image3.png" alt="" /></p>

3. **Select the token you want to 'receive'.**

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image4.png" alt="" /></p>

4. **Enter the `amount` of the token you want to pay.**

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image5.png" alt="" /></p>

5. **Confirm the `amount` of the token you will receive.**

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image6.png" alt="" /></p>

6. **Click `Swap` to proceed with the exchange and go to your wallet to sign and authorize.**

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image7.png" alt="" /></p>

7. **Transaction completed. You can click `Details` to view the details on the blockchain explorer.**

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/endless-same-chain-swap/media/image8.png" alt="" /></p>
