# Create Liquidity Pool

Create a liquidity pool and add liquidity to earn liquidity rewards and grow the value of your assets.

1. Connect your Web3 wallet.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/create-liquidity-pool/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2. Click `Create pool` on the Pool page to enter the liquidity pool creation page.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/create-liquidity-pool/media/image2.png" alt="" /></p>

3. Select the two tokens you want to include in the liquidity pool and specify the initial amounts to add.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/create-liquidity-pool/media/image3.png" alt="" /></p>

4. Click `Create pool` to create the pool, and confirm the creation details and the LP tokens you will receive.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/create-liquidity-pool/media/image4.png" alt="" /></p>

5. Click `Confirm` and go to your wallet to sign and authorize.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/create-liquidity-pool/media/image5.png" alt="" /></p>

6. The liquidity pool has been successfully created. Click `View details` to enter the pool and view the details.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/create-liquidity-pool/media/image6.png" alt="" /></p>
