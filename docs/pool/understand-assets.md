# View Liquidity Pool Assets

You can view the asset composition and share ratio of your current liquidity pool position to better understand your fund allocation and the estimated redeemable assets.

1.  Connect your Web3 wallet and select 【Pool】

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/understand-assets/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2.  Select the corresponding 【Trading Pair】

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/understand-assets/media/image2.png" alt="" /></p>

3.  Find the 【My Position】 section on the page

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/understand-assets/media/image3.png" alt="" /></p>

4.  View your assets 
    - **Liquidity**: Represents the total value of your liquidity in the current liquidity pool.\
    Below it, you can see:
      - The proportion of the two tokens in the liquidity position
      - The respective amount of each token
    - The quantity of tokens you hold in the current liquidity pool and the share of their corresponding USD value in the pool
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/understand-assets/media/image4.png" alt="" /></p>
