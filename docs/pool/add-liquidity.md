# Add Liquidity

Choosing to add liquidity will allow your assets to earn liquidity rewards. Come learn how to do it now!

1. Connect your Web3 wallet and select `Pool`.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/add-liquidity/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2. Select the `liquidity pool` you want to interact with.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/add-liquidity/media/image2.png" alt="" /></p>

3. Enter the amount of one token to add.

    - Note: The required amount of the other token will be calculated based on the current pool exchange rate.
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/add-liquidity/media/image3.png" alt="" /></p>

4. Click `Preview` to review your addition details, and confirm the amount of LP tokens you will receive and the tokens that will be deducted.

5. Click `Confirm Add` and proceed to your wallet for signature authorization.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/add-liquidity/media/image4.png" alt="" /></p>

6. Addition successful. You can check your asset status in `My position`.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/add-liquidity/media/image5.png" alt="" /></p>
