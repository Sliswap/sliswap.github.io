# Remove Liquidity

Before removing liquidity, please note:
- Removing liquidity will redeem your share of assets from the pool
- You will receive the corresponding proportion of tokens (or a single asset depending on the pool type)
- Transaction fees and slippage may apply
- Please ensure your wallet and network are correct
- Yield rewards will stop after withdrawal

Please review carefully before proceeding.

1. Connect your Web3 wallet and select `Pool`.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/remove-liquidity/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2. Select the `liquidity pool` you want to interact with and click Remove liquidity.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/remove-liquidity/media/image2.png" alt="" /></p>

3. Select or enter the proportion of liquidity you want to remove.

    - Note: Removing liquidity will be done according to the current ratio of the two tokens in the liquidity pool.
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/remove-liquidity/media/image3.png" alt="" /></p>

4. Click `Preview` to review your removal details, and confirm the amount of LP tokens to be burned and the tokens you will receive.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/remove-liquidity/media/image4.png" alt="" /></p>

5. Click `Confirm Remove` and go to your wallet to sign and authorize.

6. Removal successful. You can check your asset status in `My position`.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/remove-liquidity/media/image5.png" alt="" /></p>
