# Frequently Asked Questions

1. **What types of transactions are supported in Exchange?**\
    The Exchange feature in Sliswap supports cross-chain multi-asset swap transactions. The system automatically selects the optimal routing path to provide the best possible swap result.

2. **Which chains are currently supported for cross-chain transactions?**\
    Currently supported chains include: Endless and BSC Chain.

3. **What is the difference between Trade and traditional trading?**\
    Trade uses Liquidity Pools on the Endless chain, enabling swaps without relying on an order book matching mechanism.

4. **Why is the amount I received different from what I expected?**\
    This is due to slippage. Prices may change depending on trade size and market liquidity conditions.

5. **What is Slippage?**\
    Slippage refers to the difference between the expected transaction price and the actual executed price. It commonly occurs when liquidity is low or transaction volume is large.

6. **Are transaction fees required?**\
    Yes. Each transaction requires a small fee, which usually includes:
    - Platform protocol fees
    - Blockchain network Gas fees
    Protocol fees are charged by the platform smart contract, while Gas fees are charged by the blockchain network.

7. **What are network fees?**\
    Gas fees are transaction fees paid when executing transactions or smart contracts on blockchain networks such as Ethereum or Endless. Their primary purposes include:
    - **Paying miners/validators:** Gas fees incentivize miners (PoW) or validators (PoS) to process transactions and maintain blockchain security and operations.
    - **Preventing abuse:** Gas fees help protect the network from spam transactions and malicious attacks such as DDoS attacks.
    - **Computational resource consumption:** Different transactions and smart contract executions require varying computational resources, so Gas fees are dynamically adjusted based on computation, storage requirements, and network congestion.

8. **Why did my transaction fail?**\
    Common reasons include:
    - Slippage tolerance set too low
    - Insufficient Gas settings
    - Insufficient liquidity pool balance
    - Unsupported token

9. **How can I improve the transaction success rate?**
    You can improve success rates by:
    - Increasing slippage tolerance
    - Increasing Gas fees
    - Reducing the transaction amount per swap
