# Cross-chain Swap

Sliswap supports fast cross-chain swaps, allowing your assets to move seamlessly across multiple public blockchains.

Currently supported blockchains: Endless, BSC Chain.

1. Connect the blockchain you want to use for payment and a supported wallet.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2. Select the token you want to pay with and its corresponding blockchain.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image2.png" alt="" /></p>
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image3.png" alt="" /></p>

3. Select the token you want to receive and its corresponding blockchain.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image4.png" alt="" /></p>
    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image5.png" alt="" /></p>

4. Enter the amount of the token you want to pay.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image6.png" alt="" /></p>

5. Enter the recipient address on the destination blockchain.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image7.png" alt="" /></p>

6. Confirm the transaction, sign it with your wallet, and wait for it to be processed.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image8.png" alt="" /></p>

7. Once the transaction is successful, you can go to the order history to view detailed data. (Order lookup is currently not available for Endless same-chain transactions.)

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/cross-chain-swap/media/image9.png" alt="" /></p>
