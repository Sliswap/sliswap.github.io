# Set Slippage

Because transactions are constantly occurring in the liquidity pool, token prices and liquidity may change in real time. Setting an appropriate slippage tolerance during swaps can improve the success rate of your transactions.

However, setting the slippage too high or too low may also cause the transaction to fail.

1. Click the **`Settings`** icon to enter the slippage settings page.

    <p align="center"><img src="/docs/images/set-slippage/media/image1.png" alt="" /></p>

2. Slippage settings include: 

    - **Auto Slippage**: Dynamically recommended slippage based on market conditions and the latest trading activity. 

    - **Manual Slippage**: Manually select or enter a slippage value according to your trading preferences. 

    - Transactions will be executed based on the slippage tolerance you set.
